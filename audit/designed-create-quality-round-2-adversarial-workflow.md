## Critical findings

### R2-C1 — Sample/full identity was internally inconsistent

The candidate claimed both “calibrate before full P6/P7 expansion” and “sample/full use the same admitted IR.” Current IR compilation requires complete full-deck planning and page designs. The design must choose one model. The recommended low-risk model is one frozen complete IR, sample projection, then full render.

### R2-C2 — Re-review or severity downgrade could clear a blocker without a changed page

Current Visual Review trusts provider severity. Under the candidate, a reviewer could report Major, then omit or downgrade it on the same page hash and obtain approval.

Required control:

- stable finding identity excludes severity;
- immutable review evidence;
- workflow derives decision;
- an admitted Critical/Major remains open for the same page hash unless an immutable adjudication fact marks a factual false positive;
- a waiver may record user acceptance but cannot produce `quality_approved` for reviewed/critical work;
- re-review supersedes history, never overwrites it.

### R2-C3 — A new high-risk role could use an exception to bypass calibration

Any role absent from frozen sample coverage must invalidate expansion authorization and extend calibration, regardless of whether it claims to reuse existing grammar. Exceptions are limited to already-covered low-risk variants on controlled work.

## Major findings

### R2-M1 — Calibration lifecycle and orphan recovery were incomplete

Define one pending state:

```text
requested → render_started → sample_candidate_ready
  → office_pending → review_pending
  → approved | rework | blocked | failed
```

Each transition binds the previous fact. Resume continues the first missing step. Decision and ReferenceSet commit atomically or ReferenceSet is an idempotent derivation of an approved decision. ADR-0033 orphan closure and terminal-operation rules apply.

### R2-M2 — Invalidation mixed complete-artifact and selected-slide models

The first implementation should use a conservative full dependency key. Any Brief, Outline, Seed, Specs, Layout, Visual System, page design, asset/font, compiler, producer/capability or Office policy change invalidates the calibration. Per-slide reuse can be designed later only with an explicit dependency projection.

### R2-M3 — Risk, approval mode and automated review were ambiguous

Risk controls required evidence, while approval mode controls whether the workflow may continue without a human pause. The workflow must not silently rewrite either. An auto reviewed/critical path is permissible only with a fixed, independent, qualified reviewer; otherwise it stops for human input.

### R2-M4 — Reviewer identity and human fallback lacked a contract

Session config must bind reviewer policy and identity. Human and provider review need schema-backed request/response envelopes. Provider change is an explicit revision and invalidates review evidence.

### R2-M5 — Same-producer enforcement was only prose

The sample receipt must be generated by workflow, not self-reported by a Host response. Identity must include compiler name/version/code hash, IR hash/schema, adapter hash, Artifact Tool version, asset/font receipts and Office application/build/profile. Office pages, not Final SVG/PNG, must be the actual review image input.

### R2-M6 — Sample failure could not return to Design Direction Prototype/Seed

Earliest owner must include P4 and the pre-P5 prototype/Seed. User acceptance of a direction never waives a failed formal sample. A Seed revision invalidates direction approval, sample receipt, review, decision and reference set. A direction the producer cannot realize stops as a capability conflict.

## Minor findings

- Identical sample-page hashes may allow reuse of that page's local correctness observation, but never skip whole-candidate currentness, adjacency/cadence review or G8.
- The guarantee must be phrased as current evidence plus no admitted open Critical/Major, not as proof a reviewer cannot miss a real defect.

## Attacks the candidate already resisted

- Office unavailable remained blocking;
- hand-scripted and alternate-renderer samples were rejected in principle;
- partial byte identity did not authorize the full candidate;
- direction approval and sample approval were separate.

## Independent conclusion

The core direction is sound, but three direct bypasses and the incomplete lifecycle prevented a fail-closed guarantee. Verdict: **REWORK**.
