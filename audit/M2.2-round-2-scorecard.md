## Submodule Gate

**M2.2 Multi-format Source Adapters: PASS.**

The Production ingestion boundary now admits Markdown/TXT、HTML、PDF、DOCX、PPTX、CSV/TSV、XLSX and common raster-image metadata through one protocol, with format-native locators, bounded resources, safe OOXML preflight, immutable snapshots, explicit optional capabilities and truthful `parsed/partial` coverage. This decision authorizes M2.3 Research Planning/Runtime. It does not complete production research, Evidence normalization/conflict/freshness, block-level evidence binding, M2-wide Exit Gate or v0.5 Planning Pipeline.
