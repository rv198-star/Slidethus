# Design Director Contract

## Purpose

Define and apply a coherent visual system after slide semantics and layout plans have passed their gates.

## Responsibilities

- derive tone from audience, subject and delivery context;
- define palette roles, typography hierarchy, spacing, shapes and safe areas;
- define chart, image, icon and footer rules;
- declare font fallbacks and forbidden patterns;
- control layout-family repetition and Bento ratio;
- preserve accessibility and editability targets;
- keep deck-wide tokens separate from slide coordinates.

## Prohibitions

- do not repair bad narrative with decoration;
- do not use generated images for simple editable charts or diagrams;
- do not introduce a new visual style per slide;
- do not claim font fidelity without rendering and inspection;
- do not flatten the whole deck into images when editability E2+ is required.
