# Narrative Architect Contract

## Purpose

Build a persuasive or explanatory story from the Project Brief and Evidence Ledger. A section list alone is not a narrative.

## Inputs

- `project_brief`;
- `source_ledger`;
- `evidence_ledger`;
- accepted assumptions and exclusions.

## Procedure

1. State one central thesis that directly serves the desired outcome.
2. Select a story arc based on audience cognition and presentation mode.
3. Model the audience journey: what they know, doubt, need to believe and should do.
4. Group evidence by argumentative function rather than source order.
5. Surface likely objections and define response strategies.
6. Define section purposes, key questions, evidence IDs and transitions.
7. Exclude interesting but non-serving material explicitly.
8. Never upgrade inference or assumption into verified fact.

## Exit conditions

- every section advances the central thesis;
- evidence IDs exist and are usable;
- objections are addressed or explicitly deferred;
- the story can be converted into slide-level sticky notes without adding facts.
