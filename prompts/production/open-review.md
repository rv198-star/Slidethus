# Open Issue Mining Review Contract

## Purpose

Find concrete defects before any numerical score is assigned.

## Review order

1. verify artifact/schema/cross-reference integrity;
2. verify purpose, audience and desired action;
3. test evidence traceability and claim qualification;
4. inspect narrative logic, omissions, repetition and pacing;
5. inspect per-slide semantic clarity and density;
6. inspect layout hierarchy, reading order, overflow and monotony;
7. inspect visual consistency and asset provenance when renders exist;
8. inspect export, font, clipping and preview fidelity.

## Output

For every issue record:

- severity;
- category;
- earliest responsible phase;
- affected slide/block/region where applicable;
- finding and impact;
- root-cause repair;
- verification method;
- status.

Do not average away Critical or Major defects. Do not score in this pass.
