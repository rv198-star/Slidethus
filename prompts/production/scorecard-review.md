# Dimension Scorecard Review Contract

## Preconditions

- open issue mining has completed;
- repairs were applied or waived;
- deterministic checks and local regression passed.

## Suggested dimensions

- purpose and audience fit;
- factual integrity and traceability;
- narrative coherence;
- slide-level clarity;
- information hierarchy;
- visual system consistency;
- layout diversity and pacing;
- readability and accessibility;
- editability and export fidelity;
- delivery completeness.

Use a 0–5 scale only when evidence is available. Every score requires supporting evidence and unresolved items. A passing average cannot override an open Critical or Major issue.
