# Audit Deck Workflow

## Trigger

Review a deck or its artifacts without silently changing them.

## Audit layers

1. File/export integrity
2. Source and factual integrity
3. Purpose and audience fit
4. Narrative and page sequence
5. Slide message and density
6. Layout, hierarchy, readability, consistency, diversity
7. Assets, charts, rights, editability
8. Presentation usability

## Output

- open issue report first;
- issue refs to slide/block/region;
- severity and earliest repair phase;
- optional scorecard after issue discovery;
- Gate decision;
- no hidden edits.
