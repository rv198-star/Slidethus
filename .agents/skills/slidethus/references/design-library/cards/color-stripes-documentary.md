# Chapter-striped documentary reading

Consider for: A longer read-through report combining human stories, commitments and evidence.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use warm off-white (#FFF9F3), clear dark text and coherent chapter-color cues.
- A thin or moderately sized chapter band can orient reading, while columns separate related explanatory threads.
- Embed a few important metrics near their claims; use charts or tables when actual comparisons exceed isolated numbers.
- Real scenes and attributed quotations can anchor case pages; keep text and image regions independently readable.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard fixed chapter-to-topic colors, compulsory navigation capsules, column/card counts and all-off-white mandates. Do not inherit the source's priority for internal-style reading when the user's audience needs live presentation.

## Nearby references

Compared with fresh-brand, documentary photos and chapter strips are prominent; fresh-brand is more illustration- and rounded-module-led.
