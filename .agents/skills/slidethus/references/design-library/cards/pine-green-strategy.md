# Forest-green structure and photographic headers

Consider for: Evidence-heavy explanations that benefit from a recurring environmental image cue.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use forest green (#03522C) for structure and mint fields for breathing room, with brighter green reserved for focus.
- A darkened photographic title band can anchor a white evidence region; choose photographs for the actual subject, not necessarily technology.
- A light-gray interpretation area can accompany a main chart; fine green rules retain the same language in matrices and timelines.
- Direct labels and stage callouts connect diagrams to judgments; an edge stripe may mark section rhythm if it has a navigational role.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard mandatory photo headers, fixed stripe/column counts and industry imagery. Repetition can consume useful chart space; use headers only where needed. Numeric charts and evidence screenshots keep independent provenance.
