# Soft framing and stepwise diagnostic explanation

Consider for: Definitions, before/after mechanisms and threshold explanations.

## Borrowable grammar

- A soft pink/lilac opening can introduce a calm direction, with deep-blue text (#1C2B4A) on clear explanatory surfaces.
- Use a restrained accent rule and a stable title baseline to connect definition, mechanism and comparison pages.
- Before/after task bars should preserve real durations and event relationships; annotated wireframes can clarify a mechanism.
- Rating bands require explicit supported thresholds and text labels; keep illustrative diagrams distinct from factual product screenshots.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard the prescribed diagnostic story, fixed line/code/word counts, white-only body rules and blanket bans on real data/screenshots. Pink-purple is primarily cover framing in the source, not its entire palette.

## Nearby references

Compared with pastel-derivation, it emphasizes diagnostic before/after and threshold reading, not cumulative formal derivation.
