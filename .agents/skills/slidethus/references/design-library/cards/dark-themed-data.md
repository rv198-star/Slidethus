# Dark evidence with optional editorial labels

Consider for: Dense time-series or ranking evidence where contrast and chart typography matter.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use near-black evidence fields with green focus and consistently encoded supporting series.
- Combine an insight column with a broad plot, or use a full-width chart where the time axis needs room.
- Direct labels, event markers, units and forecast distinctions provide interpretation without hiding scales.
- Unlike the near-identical ink-green-market-trends manual, this variant permits serif-italic chart-axis/category labels while retaining sans-serif titles and body.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard crypto-specific framing, sponsor/mascot imagery, all-dark rules and automatic use of decorative series colors. Optional serif labels must remain legible, glyph-complete and supported; they are not intrinsically more elegant.
