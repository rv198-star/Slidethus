# Paper handbook with annotated visual examples

Consider for: Explanations of an object, composition or parallel visual examples.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use gray-beige paper (#EFEBE5), river blue (#246B81) and one coordinated secondary accent.
- A narrow lead-in can precede a broad example area; serif narrative and sans-serif structure distinguish reading roles.
- Connect a central sample to meaningful labels with fine leader lines; prevent crossings and clarify the object being explained.
- Photo/text bands and controlled chapter contrast can alternate quiet explanation with dense example pages.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Despite the name, river blue is the main described anchor. Discard exact sample grids, mandatory documentary imagery and pure-white bans. Numeric charts remain available when evidence needs them.
