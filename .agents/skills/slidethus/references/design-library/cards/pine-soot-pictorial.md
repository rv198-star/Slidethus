# Cinematic black-field pictorial

Consider for: Visual narratives supported by strong photography or a coherent image sequence.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use near-black fields and white type, allowing photographic color to carry atmosphere.
- Compose hard-edged image/text splits or full images with genuine negative space; mirror only when the reading path benefits.
- Unequal image frames can show facets of one subject if crop quality and alignment remain consistent.
- Strong sans-serif titles and quieter attribute labels can sustain moderate text density without competing with the images.

## Adapt, do not inherit

The source has no stable numeric-chart grammar; needed charts must be designed coherently, not omitted. Discard every-page photo mandates, fixed labels/navigation, cover accent restrictions and extremely tight title leading.
