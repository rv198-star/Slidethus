# Coral focal node and causal evidence

Consider for: A quantitative difference or input–conversion–outcome argument.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use near-black titles, neutral evidence fields and coral (#FF4D4D) at the pivotal comparison or node.
- Directly label red/gray paired charts on comparable scales; a delta note can explain one important difference.
- Let straight connectors express input, conversion and outcome; emphasis follows the relationship, not equal-weight boxes.
- Use a reversed conclusion strip only when it genuinely closes the argument; vary with open chart and table compositions.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard source-specific step counts, block percentages, vector-only preference and cover/section exclusions. Red denotes focus unless explicitly assigned a status; do not equate it with a negative result.
