# Dark instrument-style evidence

Consider for: Measured progress, status and anomaly diagnosis that benefits from strong dark contrast.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use near-black blue (#06070B), cyan data (#1FB9D5), ice-blue numbers and amber exception cues.
- Keep progress magnitude distinct from status: direct values show attainment, while a dot plus word can communicate status.
- Cross-reference chart events with numbered explanatory notes; thin rules and open space group evidence without extra panels.
- Mixed-weight titles and aligned numeric columns can maintain hierarchy; use gauges only when part-to-target progress is the actual relationship.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard all-dark and cover-only-photo rules, mandatory four-corner furniture, gauge quotas and hidden-axis mandates. Keep scales needed for honest interpretation and allow distinct colors for genuinely different series.

## Nearby references

Compared with ink-green-market-trends, this emphasizes progress/status diagnostics in cyan, rather than market-style time-series dashboards.
