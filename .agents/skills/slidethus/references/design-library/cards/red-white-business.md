# Red statements and grayscale human evidence

Consider for: A concise outward-facing message supported by people, places or a few verified facts.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use red (#CC0001), white and black/grayscale contrast; strong color can mark a conclusion or transition.
- Juxtapose grayscale scene imagery and a readable information area, preserving the subject in the crop.
- A location map or a simple trend can support one headline; direct labels should explain what the evidence proves.
- A red conclusion band or diagonal image edge may create a clear reading endpoint when the composition needs it.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard employer-brand story recipes, logo/award walls, circular triptychs and photo/chart quotas. Never invent awards, locations or institutional endorsement; retain required forecasts and source qualifications.
