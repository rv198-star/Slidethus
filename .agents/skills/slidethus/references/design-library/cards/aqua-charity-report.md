# Gallery-framed documentary and blue–yellow data

Consider for: Human-centered visual evidence, location patterns and headline measures.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use warm cream fields, blue evidence and yellow focus (#FFC907); a consistent outer mat can connect image and data pages.
- Documentary split screens or large photographs may coexist with readable text fields; an arc crop is optional, not identity furniture.
- Maps can use a pale-blue base and directly labeled priority areas; keep scale, geography and metric definitions accurate.
- Pair serif display roles with simple interior labels; dark text zones protect legibility over imagery.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard mandatory photography, fixed arc/mat counts and no-legend rules. Labels may require a legend; outlined numerals need readability checks. Do not treat yellow as positive/negative direction.
