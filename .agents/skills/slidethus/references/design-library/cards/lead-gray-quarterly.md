# Serif verdicts and combo-chart monitoring

Consider for: Recurring quantitative comparisons with an editorial newspaper character.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use black-on-white reading, deep sea blue (#06223F), teal data and restrained amber comparisons.
- Separate serif display/conclusion roles from readable chart labels and body copy.
- Column–line combinations can relate two real measures; separate charts may be clearer when units or scales conflict.
- A quiet beige footer and fine rules may unify monitoring pages while tables preserve aligned values and basis notes.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard its six-page coffee-market storyline, sample/internal-estimate values, tiny fixed pixel sizes, compulsory italic verdicts and no-photo/pie rules. Never infer a dual axis is appropriate simply because it is a signature.

## Nearby references

Compared with ebony-ledger, the emphasis is recurring combo-chart monitoring and serif verdict lines, not teal bars plus terracotta multipliers.
