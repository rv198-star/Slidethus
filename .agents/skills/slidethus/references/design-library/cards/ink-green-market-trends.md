# Dark multi-series evidence with green focus

Consider for: Dense time series, rankings or event-linked quantitative explanations.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use near-black, light labels and green focus (#A6F05F), with additional series colors assigned consistently.
- A main time series may occupy a broad evidence area beside an insight column; reduce explanations before crushing chart height.
- Place event lines, endpoint values and forecast distinctions near the relevant data; retain units and readable legends.
- Thin separators and aligned numeric columns let dark tables share the chart language.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard crypto-specific stories, mascots, sponsor/brand marks, all-dark mandates and fixed footer/header furniture. Green must not ambiguously mean both selected series and positive performance.

## Nearby references
