# Orange anchors and navy evidence bands

Consider for: Structured argument, sensitivity or forecast material with clear grouped evidence.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use warm white, orange anchors (#FF6600), deep navy headers (#172856) and lighter blue/peach annotation fields.
- Relate a main table or chart to commentary; use argument bands only where they name evidence groups.
- Distinguish history from forecast with labels plus tint or pattern, and highlight the actual base-case cell in a sensitivity matrix.
- Event callouts attach to observed points; model tables use grouped headers and subtotal hierarchy rather than decorative boxes.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard compulsory left rails, five-segment navigation, exact hue/count quotas and cover-only photography. Forecast styling does not authorize inventing projections; ensure exported hatching and labels are supported.
