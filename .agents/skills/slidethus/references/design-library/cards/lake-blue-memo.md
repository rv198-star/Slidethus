# Purple judgment and blue journal evidence

Consider for: Linked viewpoints, small multiples or scenario explanations that need editorial flow.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use deep lake-blue text (#072535), purple judgment cues (#490E5A) and blue/gray chart series.
- Open an evidence module with its conclusion and basis, then a thin rule and plot rather than a card.
- Large light-weight headings, bold labels and occasional borderless pull quotes create hierarchy; adapt portrait columns to landscape.
- Use pale grouped headers in scenario tables and subtle interval bands or inflection callouts in charts.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard mandatory macro-photo covers, giant display-size ratios, all-light body rules and fixed small-multiple counts. Use additional semantic series only when needed, and keep labels legible.

## Nearby references

Compared with honey-orange-memo, evidence sits in open editorial columns rather than navy argument bands; unlike indigo-due-diligence it need not have a judgment rail.
