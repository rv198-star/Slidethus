# Teal evidence and terracotta conclusions

Consider for: Editorial comparisons with measured authority and readable multi-chart evidence.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use white paper, black structure, teal data (#4C9F8B) and terracotta conclusion markers (#A95228).
- Pair serif headings or conclusion numerals with sans-serif chart labels, subject to actual font support.
- Historical bars can recede to gray; a nearby multiplier, bracket or arrow summarizes the change without hiding the original values.
- Reflow portrait evidence groups into landscape small multiples; independent chart heads and basis lines keep comparisons interpretable.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Despite the name, the described body is light, not dark. Discard dark-page bans, fixed two-column layouts, chart counts and unverified font availability. Do not assign terracotta negative meaning automatically.
