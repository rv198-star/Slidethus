# Photographic editorial research with blue sidebars

Consider for: Scene-rich research combining quotations, survey findings and interpretation.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use deep navy (#000735), light report blue (#C6D4F9) and modest bright-blue navigation accents.
- Place large contextual photographs beside distinct text or evidence fields, with coherent margins and careful crops.
- A deep-blue sidebar can gather a finding, attribution or short metric list; pale fields carry longer reading.
- Embed directly labeled charts within the report narrative, preserving the same type and color grammar as the photographic pages.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard travel/hotel-specific imagery requirements, fixed Part-page conventions and bans on complex quantitative evidence. Photos and quotes need truthful attribution; do not use unrelated scenery to fill a layout.

## Nearby references

Compared with travel-green-handbook, this favors photographic research, quotations and blue sidebars rather than sample-anatomy annotation.
