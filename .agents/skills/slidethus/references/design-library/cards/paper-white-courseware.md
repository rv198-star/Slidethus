# Warm-paper explanation with green results

Consider for: Approachable concepts, methods and supported case outcomes.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use creamy paper (#FDFAF5), forest-green structure (#44712E) and small coral accents (#F5987E).
- Unequal columns can connect explanation to one salient diagram; pale fields group related steps without forcing a card grid.
- A result band may tie a verified outcome to its evidence, using contrast for the result rather than decoration.
- Illustration or contextual photography can situate an abstract idea; retain plain readable label and title roles.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard required photographs, fixed result bars, exact icon-array sizes and prohibitions on quantitative comparisons. Check low-contrast blue-gray body text and use supported fonts instead of copying source substitutions.

## Nearby references

Compared with pastel-derivation, the emphasis is warm narrative and case results rather than cumulative diagram/derivation steps.
