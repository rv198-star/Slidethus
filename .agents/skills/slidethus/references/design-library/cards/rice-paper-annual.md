# Warm-paper highlights and heavy type

Consider for: Chart-led evidence with a visible thesis and expressive chapter contrast.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use warm paper (#F2EFE6), heavy black type and selective rectangular highlights behind key title words.
- Separate a concise conclusion area from the main plot; expand to paired evidence when the relationship warrants it.
- Chapter accents may vary within a reconciled palette; keep a series or status meaning stable across pages.
- Fine chart rules and direct labels maintain rigor while section-scale type and restrained color fields provide rhythm.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard compulsory chapter-color rotation, all-light body restrictions, chart/annotation counts and fixed left/right ratios. Highlights must not obscure glyphs or turn every phrase into an accent.

## Nearby references

Near xuan-paper-annual, but this description favors left-side conclusions and a large right-side chart; retain both IDs rather than treating them as identical.
