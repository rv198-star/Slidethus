# Blue structural infographic explanation

Consider for: Methods, comparisons and case explanations that benefit from diagrams and contextual imagery.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use ink text and structural blue (#4285F4), with light blue/cyan for diagram hierarchy.
- Orthogonal connectors, layered nodes and labeled treatment/control rows make mechanisms legible.
- A blue top rule and a restrained geometric edge cue can connect pages; geometry must not become repeated background clutter.
- Pair evidence with explanatory photography or screenshots only when they provide context; reflow portrait bands into landscape relationships.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard fixed icon arrays, compulsory case photos, one-chart limits and forbidden-layout lists. Icons must not imply unverified population counts; no evidence or action can be invented to fit a component.

## Nearby references

Compared with deep-blue-atlas, it admits photography, geometric anchors and colored bands rather than predominantly open linear diagrams.
