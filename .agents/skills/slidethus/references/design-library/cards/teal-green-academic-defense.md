# Teal method diagrams and comparable results

Consider for: A reproducible method or multi-panel result needing clear definitions.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use black text, teal structural connectors (#009682), sky-blue goal headers and restrained parameter highlights.
- Same-scale result panels help compare outcomes; keep axes, units and sample definitions visible.
- Thin outlined process nodes and directional arrows explain transformations; tables can emphasize a justified best value.
- A list-plus-visual relationship works when explanations genuinely point to the evidence; vary for wide tables and full-width methods.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard compulsory defense sequence, footer-dot navigation, fixed panel/grid counts and chapter-frame rules. Do not fabricate ablations, goals or equations; code/math fonts are capability-dependent.
