# Open linear reasoning with cyan evidence

Consider for: Decision trees, interval estimates, timelines and exact comparisons.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use white space, deep-blue lines (#203D74), cyan evidence points and a small exception/threshold accent.
- Let orthogonal branches and directly labeled nodes express choices; provide sufficient branch spacing.
- Horizontal-rule tables, interval lines and point estimates preserve precision without enclosing every object.
- A strong assertion and a nearby explanatory region establish reading order; lines and whitespace perform the grouping.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard all-white, no-photo, no-pie and one-object mandates. Confidence intervals need real statistical inputs; use a reference line only when its meaning is valid. Open zones do not justify excessive emptiness.

## Nearby references

Compared with blue-line-courseware, linear precision and panel-free comparisons are central, not geometric illustration or case imagery.
