# Violet documentary contrast

Consider for: Authentic scenes alternating with concise metrics and evidence.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use neutral white/near-black with violet (#5114F6) as a shared focus; surfaces may change without changing the palette's roles.
- Large square-cropped documentary images can sit beside an uncluttered statement field; keep text separate from busy textures.
- Outline-and-fill progress bars or dot ratings can explain defined scales; equal-size number circles are labels, not quantity-by-area charts.
- Use image/text asymmetry and strong type hierarchy to connect narrative pages to data-led ones.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard fixed photo fractions, mandatory section colors and the fallback to plain white when photos are missing. Re-plan the carrier explicitly; do not silently erase imagery. Chart colors follow actual comparison needs.

## Nearby references

Compared with moon-white-imagery, violet and near-black statement fields create stronger contrast while retaining documentary grounding.
