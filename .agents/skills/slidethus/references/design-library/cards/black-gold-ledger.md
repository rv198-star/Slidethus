# Grayscale evidence with a gold focus

Consider when comparisons, trends or tables carry the argument and selected judgment pages need stronger contrast. Photography is not necessary to express this grammar.

## Borrowable grammar

- Let neutral values carry most evidence while a restrained gold identifies the current subject or key threshold. Example anchors: charcoal #151515, ink #0A0A0A and gold #D6A000. Gold is an attention role, not a page-wide decoration.
- Use thin rules, aligned numeric columns and consistent units to make density orderly. A dominant chart plus a smaller interpretation/KPI rail can work; a wide comparison may instead occupy the full canvas.
- Contrast readable light evidence surfaces with selective charcoal statement or synthesis surfaces. Decide their placement from narrative, not an alternating template.
- Keep the title, numbers and chart labels in a compatible sans-serif family. Distinguish forecast, historical and uncertain quantities through explicit labels and line treatment.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Drop the source's white/dark page fractions, gold-area percentage, blanket photo/diagram bans and fixed finance story. Keep axes, scales, grids or legends wherever interpretation needs them; do not copy the source's axis-free rule or forbid a useful chart type. Dense original point sizes and table counts are observations, not minimums. Richness can come from grouping and contrast without adding unsupported numbers.
