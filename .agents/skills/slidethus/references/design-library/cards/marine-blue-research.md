# Cyan evidence with a synthesis strip

Consider for: Comparisons, bridges and matrices that need explicit explanations.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use navy headings (#00295F), cyan evidence (#00ACEE), light-blue cells and restrained orange numbering.
- Keep chart labels close to bars and changes; a small CAGR/delta capsule can relate a metric to its interpretation.
- Separate evidence from a concise synthesis strip, with fine cyan rules rather than enclosing every item.
- Grayscale still-life transitions can offer a quieter counterpoint to dense data; typography may contrast expressive headings with plain data labels.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard fixed columns, mandatory synthesis strips, tiny source font sizes, compulsory chapter photos and photo bans. Orange is not automatically positive or negative.

## Nearby references

Compared with indigo-due-diligence, it emphasizes bottom synthesis, bridge labels and cyan matrices, not a persistent side rail.
