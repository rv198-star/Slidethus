# Ivory editorial columns with amber data

Consider for: Longer explanatory reading and dense tables alongside photographic narrative.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use warm ivory (#FBF9EE), charcoal type and amber (#FDC356) for the decisive metric or period.
- Contrast sans-serif headings and numeric labels with a readable serif narrative layer where font support allows.
- Fine vertical column separators and horizontal table rules can share a rhythm; highlight relevant totals or current periods.
- Use substantial photography for narrative moments and retain the same type/color grammar on data-heavy pages.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard fixed column counts, mandatory serif fonts, all-white data sections and photo quotas. Do not inherit broken-axis charts without explicit justification and labeling; print-size tables need recomposition.

## Nearby references
