# Orange focal evidence and dark transitions

Consider for: A concise evidence sequence with occasional high-contrast pauses.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use light neutral evidence regions, orange marks (#FC7C3D) and dark type; near-black transitions can carry related orange atmosphere.
- Give the central plot or relationship diagram a clear role; align short explanation modules to the evidence they discuss.
- Use direct values and subtle gridlines, preserving the scale needed to interpret magnitude.
- Overlapping circles may describe conceptual intersection, not unverified quantitative area; transition arcs should remain subordinate to the message.

## Adapt, do not inherit

The name does not require a technology subject or glow. Discard compulsory white data pages, fixed cards/series limits, centered title rules and diagram shortcuts for precise comparisons.

## Nearby references

Compared with honey-orange-memo, it favors fewer larger anchors and stronger light/dark pauses, rather than dense navy-band modeling tables.
