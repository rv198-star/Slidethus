# Red-caption editorial evidence

Consider for: Dense arguments with chart-to-method reading, rather than a large isolated slogan.

## Borrowable grammar

- Pair black assertion captions with a small red figure cue; keep chart basis and source beside the evidence.
- Use white paper, grayscale comparisons and one red focal series (#CC2027); color directs attention rather than implying loss.
- Recompose long portrait columns into a landscape chart–interpretation relationship; a photographic section opener can overlap image and title panel.
- Thin rules and left-aligned pull quotes connect narrative and data; retain readable sans-serif roles in the target language.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard compulsory charts, fixed chart/paragraph counts, photo-only-on-openers rules and inherited navigation. Do not import print-sized labels or institutional marks.

## Nearby references

Compared with indigo-due-diligence, emphasis is caption-led red/grayscale evidence rather than a navy judgment rail.
