# Condensed assertions and colored conclusion panels

Consider for: A strong editorial assertion supported by a main chart and explanatory columns.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use deep navy (#000533), mint (#1EFF8F) and lavender accents; establish roles before varying page surfaces.
- Contrast condensed bold display titles with plain readable body text; font substitutions must preserve legibility.
- Give explanation and evidence distinct column roles; one broad chart can replace a grid of small competing plots.
- A light mint or lavender conclusion panel may gather a related judgment; dark section contrasts can reset the reading rhythm.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard four-column obligations, chart counts, body-background bans and proprietary arrow/mosaic furniture. Do not turn conclusion panels into a card wall or copy tiny print columns.

## Nearby references

Compared with rice-paper-annual, it favors condensed titles and broad pastel conclusion panels rather than rectangular keyword highlights on warm paper.
