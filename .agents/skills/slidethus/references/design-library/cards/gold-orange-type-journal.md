# Type-led visual journal

Consider for: Visual culture, specimens or case material where typography itself can carry meaning.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use strong black–white contrast, large display type and restrained neutral editorial columns.
- Case images and meaningful letterform specimens may alternate with quiet explanation; captions stay close to their source.
- A local accent such as orange (#FE9900) can support a section, but reconcile it with the whole-deck palette.
- Scale and image crop create the focal point; geometric pauses must relate to the material rather than become repeated ornaments.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard arbitrary per-page theme changes, prescribed punctuation, mandatory geometric pauses and bans on needed data charts. The source has no stable numeric-chart system; design those pages explicitly and validate their readability.
