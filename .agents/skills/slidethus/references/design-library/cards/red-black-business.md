# Red priority within modular evidence

Consider for: A mixed sequence of definitions, comparisons, priorities and roadmaps.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use neutral paper and dark type with red (#FF4D4D) marking the current stage or decisive measure.
- Light-gray modules can collect genuinely parallel content; one emphasized module should not imply equal importance elsewhere.
- Heat matrices require an explicit intensity scale; red/gray bars and near-chart captions can support comparisons.
- Vary large-type definitions, evidence plots and stage sequences within the same restrained type/color system.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard compulsory short noun titles, card counts, no-photo rules and fixed roadmap stages. Avoid treating buttons or CTAs as presentation workflow actions; maps and matrices need actual supporting information.
