# Pale report fields and black evidence contrast

Consider for: Longer arguments where dense explanation and strong quantitative evidence need different surfaces.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use pale blue/lilac fields for explanation, black evidence regions for high-contrast charts and purple for focus.
- Arrange a substantial chart beside a readable explanatory column; retain coherent labels and source placement across light and dark regions.
- Dark scene photography can introduce a question or section when meaningful assets are available.
- A consistent low-profile navigation system may orient a long report; evidence readability determines its space budget.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard the original identity mark, vertical report title, mandatory photograph types, fixed columns and industry framing. Gradients and black chart fields are optional treatments, not evidence of quality.

## Nearby references
