# Highlighted editorial assertions and broad evidence

Consider for: Dense evidence publishing with high typographic contrast.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use warm paper (#F2EFE6), ultra-heavy dark titles and restrained rectangular keyword highlights.
- A full-width chart or a broad chart beside a narrow explanation column can support the headline.
- Fine rules, direct annotations and coherent series colors keep tables and plots aligned with the editorial language.
- Section-scale color or stepped blocks may provide a pause when related to navigation; content pages need not repeat them.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard mandatory footer brands/copyright, fixed title syntax, no-image rules and section-specific color assignments. Do not copy source cases or turn colorful stacked data into a decoration.

## Nearby references

Near rice-paper-annual: both share warm paper/heavy type/highlights. This expanded manual includes broad/full-width evidence and a right explanation rail; retain distinct provenance without pretending they are unrelated themes.
