# Atlas-style relational planning

Consider for: Geography, routes or dependency structure that genuinely explains a decision.

## Borrowable grammar

- Use warm paper (#F4F0E8), navy structure, cobalt paths (#2F5BFF) and restrained vermilion coordinates.
- Let a main map, matrix or route carry the relationship, with nearby interpretations and direct node labels.
- Differentiate established, alternative and dependency paths with explicitly explained line treatments.
- Serif display roles and clean sans-serif labels can evoke an atlas without decorative compasses or map wallpaper.

## Adapt, do not inherit

Discard industry entry narratives, fixed node counts, three-market layouts and 90-day decision recipes. Do not fabricate borders, locations, distances or priority scores; label non-geographic diagrams schematic and avoid false geographic precision.

## Nearby references

Unlike a general evidence rail, this is useful only when spatial or path relationships carry meaning; palette alone can still be borrowed independently.
