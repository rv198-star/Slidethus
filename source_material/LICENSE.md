Files in this `source_material/` directory are **not licensed under the Slidethus Apache-2.0 project license merely by being present in this repository**.

This directory contains user-provided and/or third-party research inputs, cleaned excerpts, source-preserved prompts, provenance notes, indexes and derived engineering analysis. Rights in original third-party material remain with the applicable rights holders.

## Permitted project use

Slidethus keeps this material to document provenance, reproduce engineering decisions, distinguish source-derived ideas from project-authored extensions, and test source-boundary handling.

## No blanket redistribution grant

Do not assume permission to republish, sublicense, commercially redistribute, train on, or package original excerpts, images, prompts, screenshots or other third-party material outside the permissions already supplied by the relevant rights holder or applicable law.

Project-authored derived analysis may be reused under the repository's Apache-2.0 license only where it is separable from third-party source expression. When uncertain, treat the material as reference-only and perform a rights review before redistribution.

The default Slidethus wheel and Plugin bundle exclude `source_material/`.
