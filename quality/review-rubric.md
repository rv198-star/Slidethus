# Review Rubric

Score 0–5 only after open issue mining and repairs.

- 0: missing or unusable;
- 1: severe failure;
- 2: material gaps;
- 3: acceptable with visible limitations;
- 4: strong, minor issues only;
- 5: release-quality for the declared delivery level.

Dimensions: objective fit, source integrity, evidence traceability, narrative, slide clarity, hierarchy, layout diversity, visual consistency, readability/accessibility, editability/export, delivery completeness.

Every score needs evidence and unresolved items. Any open Critical/Major issue forces the applicable Gate to fail.
