## 5. M2-wide Exit contract

M2 is complete only when all of the following hold:

1. **Ingestion truthfulness** — admitted formats produce immutable, bounded snapshots with stable locator/hash lineage; unsupported/unsafe formats fail closed.
2. **Research separation** — orientation/targeted Run/Cache is resumable and provider-neutral; provider identity is stable; Result is not Source or Evidence.
3. **Evidence traceability** — Production claims bind current Source/locator/Chunk/hash and Research lineage where applicable; support/conflict/freshness/authority/use policy are explicit.
4. **Fact safety** — raw Result、high-risk Source、unsupported/disputed/do-not-use/stale binding and unqualified provisional/inference/assumption cannot silently become page facts.
5. **Gap/rework correctness** — current Outline/Specs are re-evaluated; gaps form deterministic Research handoff or formal P2 rework.
6. **Application honesty** — provider capability and disclosure permission are separate; missing capability uses explicit D3/D4/D5; requested/current/final budgets all apply.
7. **Recovery and history** — Source/Cache/Run/Gap/Application runtime facts and semantic artifacts validate current and historical references after interruption/update/invalidation.
8. **Persistent Gate** — M2 Exit validator is wired into `make verify` and Package Audit, with negative controls.
9. **No milestone overclaim** — M3–M5 remain incomplete and no MinimalImpl is relabeled ProductionImpl.
